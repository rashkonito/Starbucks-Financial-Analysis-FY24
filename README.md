# Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX) — Financial Statement & Managerial Analysis

An institutional accounting and financial statement analysis evaluating Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX) for Fiscal Year 2024. The study examines financial health, income statement common-size trends, audit findings by Deloitte & Touche LLP, managerial cost structures, and strategic leadership transitions.

### Key Financial Ratios & Performance Metrics

* **Gross Profit Margin:** Improved from **68.3%** in FY23 to **69.1%** in FY24 due to enhanced input cost efficiency and logistics sourcing.
* **Net Profit Margin:** Decreased from **11.5%** in FY23 to **10.4%** in FY24, reflecting elevated store operating expenses (+$566.2M) and G&A expense increases (+$82M).
* **Current Ratio:** Shifted from **0.78** (FY23) to **0.75** (FY24), indicating tight liquidity offset by consistent store cash flows and substantial unrecognized deferred revenues.
* **Working Capital:** Negative working capital of **-$2,222.6M** in FY24 (compared to **-$2,041.9M** in FY23). Negative working capital is typical for fast-turning retail operators with significant unearned stored-value liabilities.
* **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** **-5.21** in FY24 due to negative total equity (shareholders' deficit of -$7.44B resulting from historical share repurchases).

### Audit Analysis & Critical Audit Matter (CAM)

* **Independent Auditor:** Deloitte & Touche LLP issued an **Unqualified ("Clean") Audit Opinion** for FY2024.
* **Critical Audit Matter — Loyalty Program Rewards:** 
  * **Deferred Revenue Accounting:** Unredeemed loyalty points are recorded as liabilities rather than immediate income until goods or services are provided upon point redemption.
  * **Management Estimates & Forecasts:** Auditors rigorously evaluated breakage rates, redemption velocity, point valuation models, and unredeemed expiration percentages.

### Managerial Accounting & Operational Risk Analysis

* **Cost Classification Structure:**
  * **Variable Costs:** Coffee beans, dairy/milk products, cups, packaging materials, and direct store consumption.
  * **Fixed Costs:** Store lease obligations, manager salaries, and corporate administrative costs.
  * **Overhead Allocation:** Activity-Based Costing (ABC) allocates marketing, digital application infrastructure, and staff training across retail locations.
* **Key Operational Risks:**
  * **U.S. Store Unionization:** Ongoing union organizing drives present upward pressure on wage schedules and benefit commitments.
  * **Supply Chain & Commodities:** Volatility in green coffee bean prices, dairy input costs, and global freight disruptions.

---

### Strategic Leadership & Press Coverage

* **Executive Transition:** Appointment of CEO Brian Niccol in September 2024 (former CEO of Chipotle) spurred a 15% rebound in share price on investor growth expectations.
* **Digital Infrastructure:** Starbucks Rewards mobile platform remains a core competitive moat, driving repeat digital transactions and customer analytics.
* **ESG & Ethical Sourcing:** Commitments under the C.A.F.E. (Coffee and Farmer Equity) practices program focus on supply chain transparency and waste reduction.
